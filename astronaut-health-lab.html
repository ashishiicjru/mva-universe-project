Suit architecture

Mobility

Bearings and convolute joints at shoulder, hip, waist and ankle let crew bend, squat and pick up samples while pressurised.

Pressure protection

A sealed bladder holds roughly 30 kPa of oxygen, so crew breathe pure oxygen first to avoid decompression sickness.

Thermal regulation

Layered insulation and a water-cooled undergarment handle swings from direct sun to deep shadow.

Life support

The backpack supplies oxygen, scrubs CO₂, circulates cooling water and carries power and radio.

Digital health

Heart rate, suit pressure, oxygen and CO₂ feed a chest display and a link to the ground team.

Entanglement shot lab

Bell state, repeated measurement

Press a button to sample the circuit.

What to look for

A Bell state gives only 00 or 11, about half each, and never 01 or 10: the two qubits always agree even though each result is random. Without entanglement, all four outcomes appear about 25% of the time. Readout error leaks a few shots into the forbidden outcomes, as on real hardware.

Sampled with a pseudo-random generator, not quantum hardware.
